The Last Light

You are trapped in a pitch-black labyrinth. Your flashlight is dying. Collect 3 Golden Keys and find the heavy iron exit door to escape. Spare batteries are scattered in the dark.

Something is hunting you in the dark. Do not let it catch you.

Controls:

• W / S / A / D or Arrow Keys: Move & Strafe

• Mouse Move or Left / Right Arrows: Look Around

• E or Spacebar: Interact / Collect

• Click Canvas: Enable Mouse Look
